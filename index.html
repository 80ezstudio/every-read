Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Is Every Read free?

Yes. Tracking books and progress, saving private notes and quotes, and returning to them are free. Pro is an optional subscription for unlimited active goals, premium themes, badges, Page Partners and Community. See the current Pro benefits and eligibility above; plans and local prices are shown in the app.

Can other people see my notes?

Your private notes and quotes are for you. Community features are separate and optional.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start tracking books and saving private notes with a local profile. Pro and Community require an eligible adult account and verification.

Which devices are supported?

Every Read is available for Android through Google Play and for iPhone through the App Store. Private reading features work on iOS 18 or later. On iPhone, Community and Page Partners require iOS 26 or later, Pro, a verified adult account and Apple’s age-range check.

Where can I get help?

Email support@80EZstudio.com, or use the support and account links below.
