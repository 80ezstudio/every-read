Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Is Every Read free?

Yes. You can start tracking books, progress, notes, and quotes for free. An optional Pro subscription adds collaboration, community, more goals, themes, and badges; current pricing is shown in the app. Pro and Community currently require an eligible account aged 18 or over.

Can other people see my notes?

Your private notes and quotes are for you. Community features are separate and optional.

Do I need an account?

No. You can begin with a local profile. An account is available when you want cloud and community features.

Which devices are supported?

Every Read is currently available for Android through Google Play.

Where can I get help?

Email support@80EZstudio.com, or use the support and account links below.
