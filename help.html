Help and support

How can we help?

Every Read is designed to feel calm and clear. These answers cover the most common questions; if something is not right, contact us directly.

Do I need an account?

No. You can begin using the core reading experience locally without creating an account. Create one only when you want supported cloud backup, synchronisation and restoration.

Can I keep my reading private?

Yes. Your core library, progress, notes, quotes, ratings, goals and recommendations are part of the private experience. Social features are optional.

How do I organise a book?

Add or search for a book, then place it in Want to Read, Currently Reading, Paused or Finished. You can update progress and dates as your reading changes.

How do subscriptions work?

Every Read Pro is optional. Available plans, prices and renewal terms are shown by Google Play before purchase. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Google Play.

How do I report a problem?

Email everyreadapp@gmail.com with what happened, what you expected and your Android version. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Privacy and safety

Read the full Every Read privacy policy. Community and Page Partner experiences include privacy, blocking and reporting controls for eligible adult readers.
