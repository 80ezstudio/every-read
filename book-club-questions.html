Twenty adaptable prompts

Questions that open a book-club conversation.

Move beyond “Did you like it?” Choose two or three prompts that invite different readings rather than searching for one correct answer.

First impressions

  • What expectation did the opening create?
  • Where did your attention sharpen?
  • What surprised you about the book’s voice?
  • Which early detail mattered later?

Characters and choices

  • Whose decision did you understand but disagree with?
  • Who changed most—and who resisted change?
  • What remained unsaid between two people?
  • Which relationship carried the book?

Ideas and structure

  • What question was the book really asking?
  • How did the structure shape your response?
  • What tension did the book refuse to resolve?
  • Which idea deserves a second conversation?

Memory and meaning

  • What image will stay with you?
  • What did the book make you reconsider?
  • Which passage would you revisit?
  • What would you tell someone before they begin?

Four closing prompts

  • What changed between your first and final impression?
  • What did another reader notice that you missed?
  • Who is this book for—and who might resist it?
  • What conversation should continue after tonight?
